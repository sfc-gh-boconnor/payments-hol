# Getting Started

Welcome to the **Barclays Payments Operations Hands-on Lab**.

In this half-day lab you build a Corporate Banking Payments Operations data product on Snowflake, from Snowflake-managed Iceberg sources to governed AI agents. You follow a pathway for your role: Data Engineer, Data Analyst, Payments Ops, AI Builder or Governance.

> All data is entirely synthetic. No real customer, transaction or Barclays data is used.

---

## Step 1: Register and open the Lab Companion

### 1. Register for your Snowflake account

A dedicated Snowflake account has been provisioned for this lab. Register below and you'll receive credentials within a few minutes.

**[Register for your account →](https://go.dataops.live/EVENT_SLUG/register)**

### 2. Log in and switch role

Log in to Snowsight and switch to the role **`HOL_PARTICIPANT`**.

### 3. Open the Lab Companion

The Lab Companion is your lab guide, installed in your account. It has the step-by-step instructions and SQL, and a **Check my work** button for every module that checks what you have actually built and tracks your progress. Your facilitator will share its URL.

Choose your pathway, start with **C1**, and press **Check my work** at the end of each module.

### 4. Open the lab Workspace

In Snowsight go to **Projects > Workspaces**, click the **My Workspace** dropdown at the top left and choose **payments-hol**. It already contains the dbt project and the reference SQL. You build each module from here, pasting the Lab Companion's prompts into the **Cortex Code** sidebar.

---

## Pathways

Everyone does the shared core (C1 and C2) first, then two blocks of modules for their pathway: **Data Engineer**, **Data Analyst**, **Payments Ops**, **AI Builder** or **Governance**. The lab ends with everyone asking the **Corporate Payments 360** agent about what they built.

The Lab Companion shows the modules, order and timings for your pathway.

## What you'll need

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox or Edge)
- The Snowflake credentials from step 1

## Need help?

Raise your hand or ask one of the Snowflake team in the room. If you're stuck on a module, the facilitator can run its catch-up script so you can move on.
